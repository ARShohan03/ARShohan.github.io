I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). My research interests lie in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Medical Image Analysis.

I have experience developing attention-based architectures (RepViT, Transformers) for melanoma and brain tumor classification, as well as environmental object detection. I am the first author of a submitted manuscript on frequency-domain deep learning and was awarded a Silver Medal at NeurIPS 2025.

MeLALight: Melanoma Classification Using Multi-Stage DCT-Based Frequency-Channel Attention Fusion on RepViT

Md Abdur Rahaman (First Author)

Adaptive Transfer Learning with EfficientNetB1 for Multi-Class Brain Tumor Identification in MRI Images

Preprint

Research Projects

Environmental AI

Eco-YOLO: Marine Debris Detection with PG-EPE and YOLO11m

Developed a 40-class marine debris detector combining a novel PG-EPE enhancement pipeline with YOLO11m on the SeaClear dataset. Achieved 87.5% mAP@0.5, 89.4% precision, 80.7% recall, and 57.6 FPS on a Tesla T4 with 20.08M parameters.

GitHub
Medical AI

Diabetic Retinopathy Grading Using Hybrid CNN–Transformer

Developed an EfficientNet-B2–DeiT hybrid with adaptive fusion and imbalance-aware training for five-stage grading on APTOS and DDR datasets.

GitHub
